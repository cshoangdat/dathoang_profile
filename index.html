01 · About

Bench to 10,000 units

For 3+ years I've taken embedded products from prototype to mass production. I've finished more than ten of them, and some are now consumer devices people use every day.

The work covers the whole stack: hardware bring-up, device drivers, firmware, bootloaders, OTA updates, and the mobile or desktop app that talks to the device. I care about the parts that decide whether a product survives the field: error handling, memory budgets and power management.

I work at the toolchain level (Makefiles, CMake, linker scripts, GCC, GDB/JTAG), so a project never depends on one vendor's IDE. I'm used to working next to backend teams, including systems built on MCP (Model Context Protocol).

If you're a startup with a hardware product that has to go from "works on my bench" to "works in 10,000 units", I'd like to hear about it.
